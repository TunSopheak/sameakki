## 1. Getting started

You can start in 4 simple steps.

1. Log in with your email or username and the password your leader gave you.
2. The first time, the app asks you to change your password. Choose a new one that you can remember.
3. To change the language, tap the flag at the top right and pick "ខ្មែរ" or "English". The button next to it switches between light and dark mode.
4. Have a group code? Tap "Join a Group" and enter it. If not, wait for your leader to add you.

> **Note:** Tap the eye in a password box to see what you typed.

> **Note:** Want a different picture? Tap your name at the bottom left, then tap "Change avatar". There are 30 to choose from, including Khmer people through the ages, from the Funan kingdom and Angkor to today.

> **Note:** Tired of typing a long email? Tap your name at the bottom left, open "My settings", and set a short "Username" (for example sopheak). Next time you can log in with it instead of your email. It cannot be the same as anyone else's.

<a id="daily"></a>
## 2. Write your daily ToDo

A ToDo tells your team what you are doing today. Write it in the one box on the "Overview" or "Daily ToDo" page.

1. Write a "main point" on its own line, without "-". It can be a project name or one part of a project.
2. Under it, write the small things to tick. Start each one with "-", one per line.
3. Do not want a main point? Write only "-" lines. Each line becomes a task you can tick.
4. One project with several parts? Add more main points. The tasks under each main point are grouped together.
5. Want to show progress? Write "- 80%" at the end of the line. Already done? Add [x] or 100%.
6. As you type, a "Preview" appears under the text. Check it, then tap "Post ToDo" (or press Ctrl+Enter).

**Example**

```
Final project
Website
- Design the login page - 80%
- [x] Set up the database
Mobile app
- Build the home screen
```

**Wrong**

```
Website
- Design 80%
```

**Right**

```
Website
- Design - 80%
```

The "-" must come before the number. "(80%)" works too.

**Wrong**

```
Website
- Design 80% and then continue
```

**Right**

```
Website
- Design and continue - 80%
```

The percent must be the very last thing on the line, or it is not read.

**Wrong**

```
Website
- Design, write, test
```

**Right**

```
Website
- Design
- Write
- Test
```

Write one task per line, so each one can be ticked and counted.

> **Note:** Numbers like "1." and "2." are read as tasks too.

> **Note:** The percent of a ToDo is the average of all its tasks (a finished task counts as 100%).

<a id="fix"></a>
## 3. Made a mistake? You can edit it

You can edit a ToDo after you post it. There is no need to delete it and write it again.

1. Tap the pencil at the top right of your ToDo (on the "Daily ToDo" page).
2. Change the text, check the "Preview", then tap "Save".
3. Tasks you already ticked stay ticked if their text does not change.
4. Want to tick or untick a task? Tap it in the list.
5. When every task is ticked, the ToDo is marked as done automatically. Or tap "Mark as done" so your leader can review it.

> **Note:** Once your leader approves a ToDo, it is locked and nobody can edit it. If a change is needed, a leader can tap "Reopen to edit".

> **Note:** Want to remove it completely? Tap the bin.

<a id="tasks"></a>
## 4. Tasks given to you

Your leader gives you tasks, and you tell the team how far you are.

1. Open "Tasks" to see your tasks and their due dates.
2. Tap "Start" when you begin.
3. Drag the "Progress" bar or tap 25%, 50% or 75%. It saves by itself, so there is no save button.
4. Finished? Tap "Done" (100%) or "Done, send for review". The task then waits for your leader.
5. If it is right, your leader approves it and it is locked. If not, they tap "Request changes" and give a reason. The task goes back to "In progress" and you get a notification. Fix it and send it again.

> **Note:** Red means overdue. To ask a question, write a comment on the task.

> **Note:** Want to know what to do today? The "Overview" page has a "Today" row that shows how many tasks are "Just me", "With my team" and "Overdue". Tap one to open the Tasks page already filtered.

> **Note:** The "Tasks" and "Daily ToDo" pages have the same filters: All or Just me, Any time, Today or Dates, and status. On the Tasks page, "More filters" holds the member, category and sort order.

> **Note:** Long description? Tap "Show more" under the task to read all of it.

<a id="team"></a>
## 5. Team tasks (for everyone)

One task that every member must do, for example "Install the software".

1. When creating the task, the leader picks "Everyone" or "Choose".
2. One card shows the team's progress (the average) and each person's part.
3. You can move only your own progress. A leader can change everyone's.
4. Tap "View as individual tasks" to see each person's notes, comments and due date.

<a id="talk"></a>
## 6. Comments and reactions

1. Tap "Comments" under a task or ToDo and type in the box. Tap the arrow on the right (or press Ctrl+Enter) to send.
2. You can edit or delete your own comments. A leader can delete other people's comments but cannot edit them.
3. Tap the smiley next to a ToDo to react. Tap it again to remove your reaction.
4. The bell at the top tells you about new tasks and comments. When you read a notification, it leaves the list, so the list shows only what you have not read yet.

<a id="streak"></a>
## 7. Streaks and pokes

A streak is the number of days in a row that you posted your daily ToDo. It helps you build a good habit, and your team can see who is working hard.

1. Post a ToDo at least once a day (Cambodia time). That day counts and your streak grows by one day.
2. Your streak shows on the post box (🔥 5-day streak). The team ranking is on the "Overview" page.
3. If you miss one whole day, the streak goes back to 0. But while today is still open, you can post to keep it.
4. See someone who has not posted today? Tap "Poke" next to their name. They get a notification (and a Telegram message if they connected it).
5. You can poke the same person once a day. When they post, the Poke button goes away.
6. Want notifications in Telegram? Tap "Connect Telegram" on the ranking card (or in "My settings"), then press Start in Telegram once. This works only when the server has a bot set up.
7. After you connect, you get pokes and the same notifications as the bell (new tasks, approvals, comments), in the language you use in the app. Want pokes only? Untick "Send my other notifications here too".

> **Note:** A streak counts the days you really posted. Viewers have no streak.

<a id="leader"></a>
## 8. For team leaders

1. Create a group, then tap "Add member". The temporary password is shown only once, so copy it right away.
2. Assign work with "New task". Pick "One person", "Choose" or "Everyone".
3. Many tasks? Tap "Import tasks" on the "Tasks" page, then paste a table from Google Sheets or Excel (or choose a CSV file). One row is one task.
4. The table needs at least the columns Task title and Due date. The other columns (Module, Assign to, Members, Priority, Start date, Due time, Details) are optional. Tap "Download template" to get the right layout.
5. The app shows a preview first and skips rows with problems. Then tap the import button to create all the tasks at once.
6. Want to change a due date? Tap the date. Want to change who does the task? Tap "Reassign".
7. Review tasks marked "Waiting for review": tap "Approve" or "Request changes" (a reason is required). Many at once? Tap "Approve all". Approved tasks are locked. If a change is needed, tap "Reopen to edit".
8. Need to change a title, description, category or priority? Tap the pencil on the task card. A team task changes for everyone. Tap the light bulb on the Tasks page to read "How tasks work".
9. Open "Report" and tap "Print / PDF" to get a summary to show your lecturer.

<a id="help"></a>
## 9. Common questions

**Forgot your password?**

Tell your leader. They will create a new temporary password for you.

**Cannot log in?**

Check your email or username and your password (the password is case sensitive). If it still fails, tell your leader.

**Cannot write or edit anything?**

You may be a "viewer", who can only read. Or your leader's plan has ended, so the group is view only.

**The ToDo is approved, but you want to change it?**

Tell your leader. They must tap "Reopen to edit" first, then you can change it.

**The percent of a ToDo is wrong?**

Write "- 80%" at the end of the line and check the "Preview" before you post.

**Not getting Telegram messages?**

Check that "My settings" shows "Connected". If you blocked the bot, unblock it and connect again.
